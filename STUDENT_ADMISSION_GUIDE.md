# Student Admission Guide - GTM College

## 🎓 Welcome to GTM College Admissions!

This guide will help you through the online admission process step by step.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Create Your Account

1. **Visit the Student Portal**
   - Go to: `https://gtmcollege.edu/student/login`
   - Or click "Student Login" from the main website

2. **Register**
   - Click the "Register" tab
   - Fill in your details:
     - ✅ Full Name (as per 10+2 certificate)
     - ✅ Phone Number (with country code)
     - ✅ Email Address (you'll use this to login)
     - ✅ Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Click "Create Account"

3. **Login**
   - You'll be automatically logged in
   - You'll see your Student Dashboard

**Important Notes**:
- ✅ Use a valid email address (you'll receive updates here)
- ✅ Remember your password (write it down safely)
- ✅ Use your personal email, not a shared one
- ❌ Don't share your login credentials with anyone

---

## 📝 Step 2: Submit Your Application

### Starting Your Application

1. **From Dashboard**
   - Click the "Apply Now" button
   - You'll see a 4-step application form

2. **Important**:
   - ⚠️ You can submit only ONE application
   - ⚠️ Review carefully before submitting
   - ⚠️ You cannot edit after submission

### Application Form Steps

#### 📚 Step 1: Choose Programme

**Select Your Programme**:
- B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology)
- M.Tech (Master of Technology)
- MBA (Master of Business Administration)
- MCA (Master of Computer Applications)
- B.Arch (Bachelor of Architecture)
- Ph.D. (Doctor of Philosophy)

**Select Your Branch/Specialization**:
- Options will appear based on your programme choice
- Examples:
  - B.Tech: CSE, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, IT, AI & Data Science
  - M.Tech: VLSI, AI/ML, Structural Engineering, Power Systems
  - MBA: Finance, Marketing, HR, Analytics, Operations

**Tips**:
- ✅ Choose based on your interest and career goals
- ✅ Research the branch before selecting
- ✅ Check eligibility criteria for each programme

#### 📊 Step 2: Academic Details

**Entrance Exam**:
- Select the exam you appeared for:
  - JEE Main
  - JEE Advanced
  - TANCET
  - GATE
  - CAT
  - MAT
  - NATA
  - Other

**Entrance Score/Rank**:
- Enter your score or rank
- Examples:
  - "95.4 percentile"
  - "Rank 1200"
  - "85.6 marks"

**10+2 Board Percentage**:
- Enter your 12th standard percentage
- Example: 92.5
- Range: 0 to 100

**Tips**:
- ✅ Enter accurate scores (will be verified)
- ✅ Keep your scorecards ready for reference
- ✅ Round off to one decimal place

#### 👤 Step 3: Personal Details

**Address Information**:
- Street Address (full address)
- City
- State
- Pincode

**Guardian Information**:
- Guardian Name (Parent/Guardian)
- Guardian Phone Number

**Tips**:
- ✅ Provide complete address (for correspondence)
- ✅ Double-check phone numbers
- ✅ Use parent/guardian who can be contacted easily

#### ✅ Step 4: Review & Submit

**Review Everything**:
- Check all information carefully
- Make sure there are no typos
- Verify phone numbers and email

**Submit**:
- Read the declaration
- Click "Submit Application"
- You'll see a success message

**After Submission**:
- ✅ You'll be redirected to your dashboard
- ✅ Your application will show "Pending Review" status
- ✅ You'll receive a confirmation (if email is set up)

---

## 📱 Step 3: Track Your Application

### Your Dashboard

**What You'll See**:
- Your application card with all details
- Current status badge
- Progress tracker
- Admin messages (if any)

### Application Status Explained

#### 🟡 Pending Review
- **What it means**: Your application has been received
- **What happens next**: Admissions team will review it
- **What you do**: Wait for updates (usually 3-5 days)

#### 🔵 Under Review
- **What it means**: Your application is being actively reviewed
- **What happens next**: Decision will be made soon
- **What you do**: Check for admin messages, they might request documents

#### 🟢 Accepted! 🎉
- **What it means**: Congratulations! You're admitted!
- **What happens next**: Check admin message for next steps
- **What you do**: 
  - Read the admin message carefully
  - Check your email for admission letter
  - Pay admission fees as instructed
  - Submit required documents

#### 🔴 Not Selected
- **What it means**: Unfortunately, you weren't selected this time
- **What happens next**: Application process ends
- **What you do**:
  - Read admin message for reason
  - You can apply again next year
  - Consider other programmes/colleges

#### 🟣 Waitlisted
- **What it means**: You're on the waiting list
- **What happens next**: You'll be contacted if a seat opens
- **What you do**: 
  - Wait for updates
  - Keep checking your dashboard
  - Consider backup options

### Progress Tracker

**3 Steps**:
1. ✅ **Submitted** - Application received
2. 🔄 **In Review** - Being evaluated
3. 🎯 **Decision** - Final outcome

## 💬 Admin Messages

### What Are Admin Messages?

- Messages from the admissions office
- Appear below your application details
- Color-coded based on status:
  - 🔵 Blue: Information/Updates
  - 🟢 Green: Acceptance message
  - 🔴 Red: Rejection message

### Common Messages You Might See

**During Review**:
- "Thank you for applying. Your application is under review."
- "Please submit your 10+2 marksheet to admissions@gtmcollege.edu"
- "Interview scheduled for [date]. Details sent to your email."

**After Acceptance**:
- "Congratulations! You have been selected for [Programme]. Please check your email for admission instructions."
- "Please pay the admission fee by [date] to confirm your seat."

**After Rejection**:
- "Thank you for your interest. Unfortunately, we are unable to offer admission due to [reason]."

### What to Do When You See a Message

1. **Read Carefully**: Don't skip any details
2. **Take Action**: If documents are requested, submit them promptly
3. **Check Email**: Important details are often sent via email
4. **Respond**: If contact is requested, respond within 24 hours

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply for multiple programmes?
No, you can submit only ONE application. Choose carefully!

### Can I edit my application after submission?
No, you cannot edit. Contact admissions office if correction is urgent.

### How long does the review take?
Usually 3-5 days, but can take up to 2 weeks during peak season.

### Will I get an email confirmation?
Yes, if email notifications are set up. Also check your dashboard.

### What if I don't have an entrance exam score?
Select "Other" and explain in the score field. Admissions office will contact you.

### Can I apply if I'm still waiting for board results?
Yes, apply with expected percentage. Update later when results are out.

### Is there an application fee?
Check the admissions page for current fee structure.

### When will I know the result?
Check your dashboard regularly. Status updates are immediate.
